# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-15

**Square Enix +155% Steam surge; Capcom slides 51% as broader player declines persist.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Electronic Arts both down ~16–24% in player counts; stocks hold near recent levels (TTWO $215.41, EA $203.29). Microsoft trading $399.63. Ubisoft at $1.07 with flat stock but +12% player week-over-week—isolated bright spot among laggards.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix surges +155% to 16,335 concurrent players across tracked titles. Paradox Interactive +50% (38,301 total). Conversely, Capcom down 51% (34,155 total), Take-Two down 16% (65,131), and Embracer down 22% (57,542). Sony and Ubisoft each +12%. Sega/Atlus flat at -7%.

## Regulatory Filings
