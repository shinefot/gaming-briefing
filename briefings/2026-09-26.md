# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-26

**Paradox Interactive surges 25% on Steam; broader weakness across major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Paradox Interactive leads with +25% concurrent player growth over seven days (17,045 and 13,416 players), while Electronic Arts posts +22% growth. Embracer Group achieves +17% across two tracked titles. Stock prices remain flat to slightly down: Microsoft at $516.17, Take-Two at $201.55, and Sony at $23.55. Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.18.

Square Enix and Capcom show modest declines (−5% and −5%), while Square Enix gains +12% on one tracked title. Embracer dominates raw concurrent player counts at 62,277 and 52,220.
