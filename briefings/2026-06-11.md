# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-11

**Square Enix surges 102% on Steam as broad player declines hit most major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Electronic Arts face the steepest concurrent player drops at -24% and -31% respectively over seven days. Microsoft stock gained 3.8% since last trading session. Sony is the only top publisher with positive player momentum (+8%), while Square Enix's 102% surge is the outlier. Sega/Atlus and CD Projekt hold the largest concurrent player bases but both are declining (-10% and -12%).

## Community & Engagement
Player retention challenges across the portfolio: Take-Two (-24%), Electronic Arts (-31%), and Paradox Interactive (-23%) lead downward movement. Ubisoft and Embracer Group each lost -18% and -2% respectively. Sony's modest +8% gain and Square Enix's dramatic +102% spike stand apart from the sector trend.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K reports on May 8 and three additional 6-K filings through early June. Take-Two filed its annual 10-K on May 22 and an 8-K on May 21. Electronic Arts filed its 10-K on May 11 and an 8-K on May 5. Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-K forms within the period.
