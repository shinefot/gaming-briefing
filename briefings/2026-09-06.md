# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-06

# **Square Enix and Capcom surge +48% and +42% on Steam; Embracer slides -9%**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two leads among top publishers with 58k concurrent players (+11% over 8 days). Microsoft trades at $499.70; Take-Two at $214.69. Embracer Group, despite highest absolute player count (70k), declined 9% in the period. Ubisoft holds lowest stock price at $1.08.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix and Capcom post strongest momentum: +48% and +42% player growth respectively over 8 days, though from smaller bases (4k and 10k players). Sega/Atlus matches Capcom's growth rate (+9%) while maintaining 52k concurrent players—second highest. Embracer and CD Projekt are sole decliners in the tracker.
