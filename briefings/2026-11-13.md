# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-13

# **Capcom plummets 50% on Steam as industry-wide player declines persist**

## Market & Financials
Capcom's Steam concurrent players fell 50% over seven days—the steepest drop tracked. CD Projekt and Square Enix followed with 31% and 33% declines respectively. All tracked publishers show declining player counts, ranging from -21% (Ubisoft, Sony) to -50% (Capcom). Stock prices remain mixed: Microsoft near $524, Take-Two at $227.91, Roblox at $51.41, Ubisoft at $0.94–$0.98 (lowest among tracked companies).

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt holds the largest Steam concurrent base at ~88k–85k players despite the 31% weekly drop. Take-Two, Embracer, and Sega/Atlus cluster around 20k–27k players. Electronic Arts and Square Enix trail significantly at under 2k concurrent players each.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed 10 disclosures in 14 days, including multiple 6-K forms (most recent 2026-11-13). Microsoft submitted 5 filings, including 10-Q and multiple 8-K forms as of 2026-11-05. Take-Two and Roblox each filed 10-Q reports on 2026-11-09 and 2026-10-29 respectively.
