# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-31

**Take-Two surges +27% on Steam while Capcom tumbles -67%.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive leads Steam engagement with +27% player growth over 7 days across tracked titles, now at 52,505 and 40,290 concurrent players. Stock rose to $235.39. Capcom and Electronic Arts face sharp declines: Capcom down -67%, EA down -39%. Microsoft (MSFT) holds steady at $513.53; Ubisoft stock remains depressed at $1.18.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and CD Projekt titles show mid-single-digit erosion (-9% and -10% respectively). Sega/Atlus flat against trend with +6% growth. Paradox Interactive, Ubisoft, and Square Enix all posting minor losses. Lowest engagement: EA (1,299 and 941 players), Square Enix (2,741 and 1,889).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony leads filing activity with four 6-K submissions in the past two weeks (07-31 through 08-12). Take-Two filed 10-Q and 8-K on 08-07. Microsoft, Roblox, and EA each submitted 10-Q or annual filings late July through early August. No material disclosures flagged in titles.
