# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-04

**Embracer Group surges 17% in concurrent players; Square Enix slides 20% week-over-week.**

## Market & Financials
Stock prices are flat across the board. Microsoft ($390.53), Take-Two ($255.03), and Electronic Arts ($205.25) remain stable. Square Enix ($16.00) and Ubisoft ($1.23) show no price movement despite player losses.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads Steam with 94,565 concurrent players (+8%). Embracer Group posts the strongest growth at +17% across two titles, while Square Enix faces the steepest decline at -20%. Sega/Atlus and Paradox Interactive both down 7%; Capcom off 3%. Electronic Arts barely holds steady at +3%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six documents in the past 14 days, including a 20-F annual report (June 18). Take-Two submitted a 10-K (May 22) and 8-K (May 21). Roblox filed an 8-K (May 29). No new filings from Microsoft, Electronic Arts, or other tracked publishers in this window.
