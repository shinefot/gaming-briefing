# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-13

**Embracer Group leads Steam concurrent players with +15% weekly surge; EA drops 25% as broader engagement softens.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group tops concurrent player rankings at 45K–37K across tracked titles, the only major publisher posting double-digit growth (+15% over 7 days). Take-Two (31K–30K, +2%) holds third place. Stock prices mostly stable: Microsoft at $545.39, Take-Two at $208.82, Roblox at $49.79. Ubisoft shares lowest at $1.07.

## Community & Engagement
Engagement splits sharply: Embracer and Take-Two gain; CD Projekt (-5%), Capcom (-18%), Square Enix (-18%), and Electronic Arts (-25%) all post weekly declines. EA shows the steepest drop among tracked publishers. Sega/Atlus and Paradox Interactive hold flat. Lowest concurrent players: Ubisoft at 3K, Electronic Arts at 1K.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-Ks and one prior (Sept 3–Oct 5). Take-Two issued two 8-Ks (Sept 22–30). Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept 2). No material news disclosed in summary review.
