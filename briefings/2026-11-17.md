**Steam player base down across all tracked publishers; Embracer and CD Projekt hit hardest.**

CD Projekt and Embracer Group show steepest 7-day declines at –26% and –23% respectively on Steam. Take-Two trades at $213.47 (up from $203.93 in prior snapshot); Microsoft at $517.99 (up from $506.24). Ubisoft stock flat at $0.90; most other holdings stable or minor shifts.
