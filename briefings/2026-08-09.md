**Square Enix surges +27% in Steam engagement; Take-Two and Capcom decline sharply.**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads at $499.99; Take-Two ($246.50) and EA ($209.70) command mid-tier valuations. Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.19. All price points unchanged from prior snapshot.

## Community & Engagement

Take-Two (-9%), Capcom (-11%), and Sony (-14%) posted the steepest declines. Paradox, Ubisoft, and Sega/Atlus also negative. Only EA and CD Projekt gained alongside Square Enix.
