# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-08

**Square Enix surges 41% on Steam; Embracer and CD Projekt decline double digits.**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix player count jumped +41% over 8 days, the strongest gain tracked. Capcom also grew +18%. By contrast, Embracer Group and CD Projekt both fell -12% and -11% respectively. Stock prices show minimal movement today: Microsoft at $499.74, Take-Two at $214.80, EA at $209.70. Roblox shifted from $43.32 to $45.55.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer leads concurrent players (65,796), followed by Take-Two (52,177). Sega/Atlus held steady (-2%), while Paradox Interactive declined -4%. Ubisoft, EA, and Sony occupy the lowest tiers (under 3,300 concurrent players each). Square Enix and Capcom, despite smaller absolute player bases, posted the period's sharpest gains.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K forms (most recent 2026-09-03). Microsoft submitted two 8-K filings and one 10-K. Take-Two filed 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-08-07. Roblox issued 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-07-30.
