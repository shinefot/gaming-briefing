# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-26

**Take-Two surges +44% on Steam as CD Projekt and Ubisoft slide sharply.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two leads engagement growth at +44% over six days (54.7K–51.6K concurrent players), while Paradox Interactive follows at +34%. CD Projekt and Ubisoft face the steepest declines at -23% and -25% respectively. Stock prices: Microsoft $489.93, Take-Two $227.34, Roblox $55.63; Ubisoft trades lowest at $0.64–0.69.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt holds the largest player base (62.3K), but momentum is negative. Take-Two's growth stands out against broader weakness—Capcom (-17%), Sega/Atlus (-9%), and Electronic Arts (-3%) all retreating. Sony and Square Enix post modest gains (+13%, +18%) at much smaller player counts.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports since October 30; Microsoft and Take-Two each filed 10-Qs on October 28 and November 9 respectively. Activity concentrated among the three largest players by market cap.
