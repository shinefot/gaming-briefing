# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-03

# **Capcom posts 116% player surge; CD Projekt gains 25% while Embracer and Sega decline**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads by valuation at $517.53; Take-Two at $203.10. Capcom stock rose to $14.05 as concurrent players jumped 116% over 7 days (8,691–15,693 peak). CD Projekt gained 25% in player count; Ubisoft stock slipped to $1.08–$1.11 with 7% player decline.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group's largest title dropped 9% (53,721 players). CD Projekt's two tracked titles both grew 25% (35,477 and 49,053 peak). Capcom's two games together serve 24,384 concurrent players. Sony titles up 23%; Electronic Arts up 14%; Sega and Square Enix each down 3%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms (Sept. 3 and 14). Take-Two filed two 8-K forms (Sept. 22 and 30). Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept. 2). No material details disclosed in this briefing data.
