# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-07

**CD Projekt Steam traffic surges 11% week-over-week as Square Enix tumbles 12%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads Steam concurrent players at 85,390 (+11% over 7 days), with Take-Two second at 42,970 (+8%). Square Enix posts the steepest decline at −12%, while Ubisoft's +13% growth stands out among laggards. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $393.06; Take-Two (TTWO) at $258.75; Ubisoft (UBSFY) at $1.25—lowest among tracked stocks.

## Community & Engagement
Paradox Interactive (−9%), Embracer Group (−7%), and Capcom (−4%) show weakness on Steam. Sega/Atlus flat (−1%); Sony unchanged. EA trails the field at 2,114 concurrent players (−4%). CD Projekt and Ubisoft are the week's only gainers beyond low single digits.
