# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-27

**CD Projekt's Steam presence surges 154% in a week, while Capcom slides 27%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt player count jumped to 146,377 concurrent (+154% over 7 days), the largest gain among tracked publishers. Conversely, Capcom dropped 27% to 5,229 players—the steepest decline. Most other studios posted single-digit losses. Stock prices showed modest movement: Take-Two at $202.53, Roblox at $54.58, and Ubisoft near $0.93.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and Take-Two each lost roughly 15–20% of concurrent players over the week. Sega/Atlus, Paradox, and Sony held losses under 6%. Electronic Arts and Ubisoft remained relatively stable at the lower end of tracked concurrent player counts.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Oct 5 and Oct 14) and one earlier filing (Sept 14). Take-Two submitted two 8-K filings (Sept 30 and Sept 22). No material details provided in filing summaries.
