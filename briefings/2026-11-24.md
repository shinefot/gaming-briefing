# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-24

**Take-Two surges 80% in Steam concurrent players; Square Enix posts strongest six-day gains at 162%.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive leads player engagement with 44,547 concurrent users (+80% over 6 days) while trading at $220.41. CD Projekt holds the largest player base at 59,007 but faces a 19% decline; stock at $16.44. Square Enix (+162%) and Sony (+84%) show the sharpest momentum despite smaller absolute player counts (3,886 and 3,812 respectively). Ubisoft continues to underperform across both metrics: 2,005 players (-26%) and $0.65 stock price.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt, Embracer, and Capcom all declined over the six-day window. Paradox Interactive (+36%), EA (+41%), and Sega (-8%) occupy the middle tier of engagement change. Take-Two's sharp player surge stands out among major publishers tracked.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony dominates recent SEC activity with nine 6-K filings between October 30 and November 13. Microsoft filed two 8-Ks, one 10-Q, and one additional 8-K (October 28–November 5). Take-Two and Roblox each filed one 8-K and one 10-Q in late October/early November.
