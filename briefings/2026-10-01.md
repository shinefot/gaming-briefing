# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-01

**Embracer, Ubisoft, and Sony see Steam presence collapse to zero; Capcom down 37% week-over-week.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive stock declined 2.3% to $203.31 following two recent 8-K filings (Sept 22, Sept 30). Microsoft holds at $516.53; Roblox steady near $42.53. CD Projekt, Sega/Atlus, and Ubisoft all down 2–4% from stated ranges.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group, Ubisoft, and Sony report zero concurrent Steam players (down 100% week-over-week). Capcom shows steepest decline among active titles at –37% to 5,261 players. Paradox Interactive's portfolio holds relatively steady at –4% (12,950 players). Take-Two and CD Projekt in mid-tier range at –10 to –13%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms (Sept 3, Sept 14); Microsoft one 8-K (Sept 2). Take-Two's two recent 8-Ks coincide with stock decline. No other major filings in the past 14 days across tracked companies.
