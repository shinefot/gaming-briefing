# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-14

**CD Projekt sees Steam engagement drop 17% over 8 days; Embracer posts strongest growth at +15%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt and Take-Two lead concurrent player counts (72K and 44K respectively), but CD Projekt faces the steepest decline at -17% over 8 days. Embracer Group's titles are the only major winner, up 15% to 38K players. Stock moves are modest: Microsoft up 1.2%, Take-Two up 1.7%, Embracer down 4.3%; Ubisoft flat at $1.28–$1.29.

## Community & Engagement
Sega/Atlus, Paradox, Capcom, and EA all show single-digit declines. Sony's titles posted the sharpest player drop at -24% (3.8K–4.3K concurrent), followed by Square Enix at -22%. Only Embracer and Take-Two grew engagement over the 8-day window.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed seven 6-K forms between June 3 and July 14, plus one 20-F on June 18. Microsoft filed a single 8-K on June 5. No material details provided in today's snapshot.
