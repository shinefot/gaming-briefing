# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-03

**CD Projekt surges 12% on Steam while Take-Two and Square Enix lose ground.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads Steam engagement with two titles at 76,147 and 64,206 concurrent players, both +12% over seven days. Take-Two's titles dropped 13% (43,000 and 35,107 players). Square Enix fell 17% to 3,180 and 2,170 players—the steepest decline tracked. Electronic Arts reversed course with +22% growth across two titles (2,196 and 1,849 players), though from a smaller base. Stock prices show mixed movement: Microsoft +4.9%, Take-Two +2.2%, EA flat. Roblox, Sony, and CD Projekt held steady.

## Community & Engagement
Sega/Atlus (42,102 and 35,271 players) and Embracer Group (40,122 and 33,042) hold mid-tier positions, each declining or flat over the week. Paradox Interactive and Capcom both fell 8–9%. Ubisoft and Sony titles occupy the lowest player counts (under 4,300), both with minor declines.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent: July 31). Microsoft filed 8-K and 10-K on July 29. Roblox and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and quarterly updates on July 30. No material disclosures summarized in provided data.
