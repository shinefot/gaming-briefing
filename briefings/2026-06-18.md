# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-18

**Square Enix posts +181% Steam gain in 8 days; Sony declines -12%.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive stock rose to $241.68, while Microsoft held steady around $377–$384. Square Enix and Capcom posted modest gains ($16.00, $9.09 respectively). Roblox edged up to $52.42; Ubisoft remained under $1.12.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix led growth at +181% concurrent players over 8 days, followed by Paradox Interactive (+71%) and Capcom (+47%). Embracer Group and Electronic Arts both gained +29% and +28%. Sony's titles fell -12%—the only major decline tracked. Sega/Atlus held flat at +0%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports and one 20-F (annual) in the last two weeks. Take-Two, Microsoft, and Roblox each filed multiple 8-K notices. Electronic Arts submitted a 10-K on May 11.
