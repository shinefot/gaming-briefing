# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-07

**CD Projekt leads Steam engagement with +6% growth; Square Enix and EA surge despite smaller player bases.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt commands the largest concurrent player pool at ~65,600, up 6% over 8 days. Microsoft (MSFT) trades highest at $502.50; Ubisoft (UBSFY) lowest at $1.17. Take-Two (TTWO) down 8% on Steam but stock holding at $233.12.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix and Electronic Arts show strongest momentum: +20% and +16% respectively, though from smaller bases (~2,700 and ~2,100 players). Conversely, Sony (-12%), Take-Two (-8%), Paradox (-9%), and Ubisoft (-8%) all declined. Embracer and Sega/Atlus flat.
