# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-16

**Square Enix player engagement drops 23% in a week; EA posts rare 37% gain.**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix Steam concurrency fell 23% over seven days (1,874–2,001 players), the steepest decline across tracked publishers. EA bucked the trend with a +37% weekly gain (1,085–1,204 players). Stock moves were muted: MSFT $499.63, TTWO $215.19, EA $209.70. Roblox (RBLX) fell to $50.65 from $48.50.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer leads concurrent players (39,558–41,813) but shed 8% over seven days. Take-Two dropped 16%, the second-largest decline. Capcom and Sony showed relative stability (−1%). Paradox Interactive and CD Projekt each lost 7–9%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-Ks in the past two weeks (Sept. 3 and Sept. 14) plus three earlier filings. Microsoft and Take-Two each submitted one 8-K and one 10-Q during the period. No material disclosures detailed.
