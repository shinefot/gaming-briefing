# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-29

**CD Projekt posts only gain as Steam engagement drops across most publishers.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt alone grew +3% over 7 days to ~57,500 concurrent players. Take-Two, Square Enix, and Electronic Arts each fell -15%—the steepest declines tracked. Stock prices moved modestly: Microsoft +0.7%, CD Projekt +5.5%; Ubisoft and Sega/Atlus dropped 6%+ each.

## Community & Engagement
Take-Two and Square Enix led Steam declines at -15%; Sega/Atlus, Paradox, and Capcom fell 4–5%. Ubisoft, Sony, and EA saw 7–8% drops. CD Projekt's modest growth stands apart in a generally declining week.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed eight 6-K and 20-F reports over the past 14 days (June 18–July 17). No other publisher filings noted in this window.
