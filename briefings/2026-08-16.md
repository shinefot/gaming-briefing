# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-16

**Embracer surges 132% on Steam; EA and CD Projekt slide sharply**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group's Steam concurrent players jumped 132% over 8 days to ~93k, leading all tracked publishers. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $495.40; Take-Two (TTWO) at $246.95. Ubisoft gained 17% on Steam but remains lowest-priced at $1.22/share.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt dropped 19% to 60–65k concurrent players. Electronic Arts fell 21% to ~1.9k (lowest among tracked). Capcom lost 15%; Square Enix down 12%. Sega/Atlus and Paradox Interactive held relatively flat (−1%). Ubisoft's 17% gain to ~4.3k reverses broader weakness.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent: 2026-08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, EA, and Take-Two each filed quarterly (10-Q/10-K) and event reports (8-K). No material disclosures flagged in filing headers.
