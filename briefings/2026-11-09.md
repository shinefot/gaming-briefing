# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-09

**Broad Steam player decline across all tracked publishers; Embracer and Sega hit hardest.**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads at $545.18; Take-Two at $214.87. All 20 stock prices shown held flat or moved marginally today—no major movers. Ubisoft remains lowest at $0.94.

## Community & Engagement
Steam concurrent players dropped across the board over 7 days. Embracer Group and Sega/Atlus saw the steepest declines (-36% and -19% respectively). CD Projekt's two titles each fell 27%; Electronic Arts and Capcom both down 24%. Square Enix showed the mildest drop at -8%.

Take-Two and Roblox each filed 10-Qs alongside 8-Ks in late October/early November. No material event disclosures stand out from the filing types alone.
