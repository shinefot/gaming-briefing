# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-25

**CD Projekt and Square Enix see steepest Steam player declines as Capcom gains ground.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt's Steam concurrency dropped 18% over seven days (60,225 players), the largest decline among tracked publishers. Square Enix fell 24% to 3,471 players. Capcom is the only major publisher with growth: +5% to 14,379 players. Stock prices remain largely flat; Microsoft trades at $383.78, Take-Two at $231.80, Electronic Arts at $209.10.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group maintained flat engagement at 35,884 players. Take-Two and Paradox Interactive each saw moderate 10% and 9% declines respectively. Ubisoft and Sony posted double-digit drops (−13% and −19%), with Sony's concurrent count falling to 3,521 players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed nine 6-K reports (foreign issuer current events) and one 20-F (annual report) in the past 14 days, concentrated in mid-to-late June. No other major publishers filed material disclosures in this window.
