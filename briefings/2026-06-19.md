# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-19

**Paradox Interactive surges 72% in Steam concurrent players; CD Projekt and Square Enix also post strong gains.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads current concurrent players at 47,498 (+44% over 8 days), followed by Sega/Atlus at 38,554 (−3%). Paradox Interactive posts the steepest 8-day climb at +72%, while Electronic Arts (+59%) and Square Enix (+57%) also show sharp upticks. Take-Two and Sony decline (−4% and −12% respectively). Stock prices show modest movement; Microsoft trades $379.44, Take-Two $241.68, EA $203.31.

## Community & Engagement
Ubisoft trails all tracked publishers at 3,290 concurrent players (−2% over 8 days). Capcom holds 20,959 players (+9%). Multiple titles show duplicate entries, suggesting portfolio breadth across all major publishers on Steam.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony leads filing activity with five 6-K forms and one 20-F since May 8. Roblox filed two 8-Ks (May 19 and May 29). Microsoft and Take-Two each filed one 8-K; Take-Two also submitted a 10-K on May 22. Electronic Arts filed a 10-K on May 11.
