# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-23

**CD Projekt surges 93% on Steam while Capcom plummets 52%—broadest player decline across tracked publishers.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads concurrent players at 115,637 (+93% week-over-week), while Capcom faces the steepest drop at -52%. Take-Two Interactive down 27% despite stock holding above $206. Ubisoft trades near $1.00, lowest among tracked equities. Microsoft (MSFT) at $524.48; Roblox stable at $53.16–$53.48.

## Community & Engagement
Eight of nine tracked publishers show negative 7-day Steam trends. Embracer Group (50,209 players, -9%) and Paradox Interactive (-26%) face moderate declines. Sega/Atlus down 30%; Sony and Square Enix each down ~32–46%. Only CD Projekt's two titles register growth.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed three 6-K forms (Oct 14, Oct 5, Sept 14); Take-Two issued two 8-K filings (Sept 30, Sept 22). No other publishers reported SEC activity in the past 14 days.
