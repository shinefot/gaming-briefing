# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-24

**CD Projekt's 135% player surge dominates Steam; EA posts strongest 8-day growth among tracked publishers.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads concurrent players at 66,943 (+135% over 8 days), far ahead of Sega/Atlus (36,773, +8%). Electronic Arts shows the second-sharpest growth at +78%, though from a smaller base of 2,262 players. Sony and Ubisoft declined over the period (−15% and −4% respectively). Stock prices are mixed: CD Projekt up to $15.35, but Sony down to $19.57.

## Community & Engagement
Capcom (+17%), Take-Two (+13%), and Square Enix (+13%) all posted double-digit player gains. Paradox Interactive's +1% growth is the weakest positive mover. Embracer Group is the only major publisher besides Sony and Ubisoft in the red (−6%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K reports and one 20-F in the past 14 days. Take-Two and Electronic Arts both submitted 10-K filings (May 22 and May 11, respectively). Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-K forms.
