# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-29

**CD Projekt Steam players surge 15% as broader Steam portfolio sees sharp pullbacks across most major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads with +15% player growth over 7 days (136,882 concurrent). Stock flat near $17. Take-Two down 2.7% to $192.22 despite 28,062 Steam players; Capcom stock fell 5.6% to $12.13 as its Steam base contracted 51% week-over-week. Microsoft (MSFT) holds $527.92; Roblox (RBLX) down 2.8% to $51.59.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and Square Enix both dropped 31% Steam players over 7 days. Paradox Interactive, Sega/Atlus, and Take-Two each saw declines between 19–23%. Ubisoft and Sony posted smaller 8% drops. Only CD Projekt showed upward momentum in the tracked cohort.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Oct 5, 14). Microsoft submitted paired 8-K and 10-Q filings on Oct 28; Take-Two filed two 8-Ks (Sept 22, 30). No material disclosures noted in available summaries.
