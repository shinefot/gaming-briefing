# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-10

**Square Enix Steam players down 38% in a week; broad concurrent-player decline across tracked publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix stock flat at $16.00 despite the steepest player drop in the tracked set. Take-Two up 3.3% to $252.22 with more moderate 10% player decline. Microsoft (MSFT) at $383.25; EA down 0.6% to $206.42 despite 25% player fall.

## Community & Engagement
Sega/Atlus, Paradox Interactive, and Capcom each lost 19–24% of concurrent Steam players over seven days. CD Projekt and Embracer Group at ~80K and ~35K players respectively, both down 15–16%. Ubisoft smallest in this set at 3,390–3,463 players, -10%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent 07-06), plus a 20-F on 06-18. Microsoft filed one 8-K (06-05). Roblox filed one 8-K (05-29). No material details provided.
