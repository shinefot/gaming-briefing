# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-08

# **CD Projekt and Capcom lead Steam momentum; Square Enix player base contracts 16% in 8 days**

CD Projekt commands the highest concurrent players (85,390) with +10% growth over 8 days, followed by Take-Two at 43,019 (+8%). Stock moves are modest: Microsoft up 2.6% to $393.06; Take-Two up 2.4% to $258.75. Square Enix shares flat at $16.00 despite -16% player decline. Ubisoft remains lowest-priced at $1.33, up 6.4%.

## Community & Engagement
Capcom shows strongest growth momentum at +9% (16,843 players), matching CD Projekt's rate. Paradox Interactive faces steepest decline at -8% (17,257 players). Embracer Group down 3%; Sega/Atlus flat at -1%. Electronic Arts and Sony register minimal movement (+2% and -4%, respectively).

## Regulatory Filings
