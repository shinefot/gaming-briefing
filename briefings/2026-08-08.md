# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-08

# **Square Enix Steam traffic surges 21% while Take-Two and Capcom face double-digit declines**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two stock climbed 5.8% ($232.91 → $246.50) despite Steam concurrent players falling 11% over 8 days. Capcom shares gained 5.9% ($12.39 → $13.12) with a matching 14% player decline. Square Enix stock held flat at $15.45 while Steam traffic rose 21%—the only major gainer in player counts. Microsoft (MSFT) and EA remained steady; Roblox dipped 2.9%.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix's 21% 8-day player growth stands alone among tracked publishers. Take-Two, Capcom, Paradox, and Sony all posted double-digit declines (11–14%). Embracer and Sega/Atlus were flat to -6%. Ubisoft recorded the lowest absolute concurrent player count at 1,073 total (two titles tracked, both -6%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed 6 updates in 14 days; Microsoft, Roblox, and EA each filed 8-K and quarterly reports. Take-Two submitted 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-08-07. No material event disclosures flagged in titles or dates provided.
