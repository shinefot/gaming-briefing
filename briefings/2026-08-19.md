# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-19

**Embracer and EA face sharp player declines; Ubisoft gains ground on Steam.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group and Electronic Arts lead declines, each down 44–49% in concurrent players over 8 days. CD Projekt follows at -21%; Square Enix at -26%. Ubisoft stands alone with +7% growth. Stock prices show modest movement: Microsoft at $481.38, Take-Two at $243.99, EA at $209.70. Embracer trades at $8.00, Ubisoft at $1.23.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer commands the largest Steam footprint at ~99k–74k concurrent players, though the sharp 8-day drop is the steepest among major publishers tracked. CD Projekt holds second place at ~51k–49k. Paradox Interactive remains flat (±0% over 8 days). Take-Two, Sega/Atlus, and Capcom all show single-digit to low-double-digit declines.
