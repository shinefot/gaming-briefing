# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-18

# **Capcom and EA surge on Steam while Take-Two and Square Enix decline sharply**

## Market & Financials
Capcom leads with +21% player growth over 8 days (9,891 and 6,052 concurrent), while Electronic Arts posts the largest absolute gain at +63% (1,809 and 1,189 players). Take-Two and Square Enix both fell -13% over the period. Stock prices: Microsoft $493.65–$494.72; EA $209.70; Take-Two $206.35–$210.23; Capcom $13.82–$14.12.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the highest concurrent count (45,930 and 41,709 players, each -4%). Sega/Atlus and CD Projekt both show -5% to -7% declines. Ubisoft remains lowest at 1,189–3,022 players but gained +11% over 8 days.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Sept. 3 and Sept. 14); Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept. 2). Take-Two submitted both an 8-K and 10-Q on Aug. 7. No other tracked publishers filed in the last 14 days.
