# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-12

# **Embracer Group surges 348% on Steam in 8 days; most peers decline**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group's Steam concurrent players jumped 348% over eight days (146.7K peak), while most competitors posted declines. Take-Two (-6%), Capcom (-9%), and Sony (-12%) saw the steepest drops. Square Enix (+29%) and EA (+11%) posted gains. Stock-wise: MSFT $501.94, TTWO $254.44, and EA $209.70 lead by price; Ubisoft at $1.18 is lowest-priced tracked stock.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and CD Projekt command the largest Steam audiences (146.7K and 68.4K concurrent). Sega/Atlus (35.4K) trails significantly. Square Enix and EA are only gainers beyond Embracer's spike. Ubisoft and Sony occupy the smallest concurrent player bases among tracked publishers.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed seven 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent Aug 12). Microsoft, Roblox, EA, and Take-Two each filed dual 8-K/10-Q disclosures. No material announcement flags visible from filing types alone.
