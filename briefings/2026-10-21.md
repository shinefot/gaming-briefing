# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-21

**CD Projekt surges 49% in weekly player growth; Capcom slides 24%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt stock climbed to $17.02, tracking a 49% surge in Steam concurrent players over seven days—the sharpest growth in the tracked set. Take-Two holds $207.25 with modest +3% player movement. Capcom stock fell to $14.33 amid a 24% weekly player decline. Microsoft (MSFT $533.59) and Roblox (RBLX $52.95) show minimal intraday shifts.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and CD Projekt dominate Steam concurrent counts. Capcom, EA, and Square Enix all posted double-digit player declines over seven days. Ubisoft remains at the lowest concurrent-player position among tracked publishers (2,897). Sega/Atlus and Paradox each saw minor declines (-3% to -4%).

## Regulatory Filings
