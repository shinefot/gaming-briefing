# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-19

**EA and Capcom surge on Steam while Take-Two and CD Projekt falter.**

## Market & Financials
Electronic Arts posts the sharpest momentum: +84% concurrent player growth over 7 days, with stock holding at $209.70. Capcom follows at +45% player growth ($14.12 stock). Take-Two and CD Projekt both face headwinds: -15% and -11% player declines respectively, with TTWO at $206.35 and CD Projekt at $15.74. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $493.78, largest by market cap among tracked firms.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group leads absolute concurrent player count across its titles (53,944 and 45,930), though both flat (+1% trend). Sega/Atlus holds third-tier positions (42,707 and 36,453 players, both -7%). Sony shows weakness: -20% player drop across two titles (2,679 and 2,145 players). Square Enix outperforms trend among smaller players (+28% growth to 4,166 and 2,310).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-Ks within 14 days (Sept 3 and Sept 14). Take-Two submitted both 8-K and 10-Q on Aug 7. Microsoft filed a single 8-K (Sept 2). No other major filers in the window.
