# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-11

**Square Enix surges +64% in Steam players; Embracer and EA also gain ground.**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads stock valuations at $508.53; Take-Two at $254.44 despite -4% player decline over 8 days. Sega/Atlus stock dropped to $4.00 (down from $4.34), lowest among tracked publishers. Ubisoft at $1.19 remains under pressure.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix posts the sharpest player growth at +64% (3,467 concurrent). Embracer Group and Electronic Arts both +19%. Conversely, Sony (-11%), Ubisoft (-9%), Take-Two and Capcom (-4%) all shed players. Sega/Atlus flat at +0%. CD Projekt and Paradox remain modest at +5% and -2%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K forms in the past 14 days, most recent 2026-08-11. Microsoft, Roblox, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and 10-Q/10-K pairs late July through early August. Take-Two filed 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-08-07. No material event disclosures noted in filing titles.
