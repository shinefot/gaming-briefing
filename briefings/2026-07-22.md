# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-22

**Take-Two gains ground while CD Projekt and Square Enix post double-digit Steam declines.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two's Steam concurrent players rose 6% over seven days to ~44,800—the strongest performer among tracked publishers. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $400.57; Take-Two (TTWO) at $236.93. Ubisoft (UBSFY) remains lowest-priced at $1.18, with stock flat.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads in absolute Steam concurrency (~59,000 players) but posts the steepest 7-day decline at -10%. Square Enix and Sony each fell -12% and -11% respectively. Sega/Atlus (+4%), Take-Two (+6%), and Ubisoft (+5%) show gains; Capcom holds flat at +1%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed nine 6-K reports and one 20-F over the last 14 days, indicating active disclosure activity. No material items noted in filing titles.
