# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-13

**Square Enix surges 121% on Steam in one week; Take-Two and Paradox decline sharply.**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix leads Steam engagement with a +121% weekly surge across its titles, while Take-Two and Paradox Interactive both post -8% and -9% declines. Stock prices remain relatively flat: Microsoft at $390.76, Take-Two at $211.77, and Electronic Arts at $203.29. Ubisoft trades at $1.08, lowest among tracked publishers.

## Community & Engagement
Sega/Atlus holds the largest concurrent player base at 47,917 (down 4% weekly). CD Projekt shows resilience with +3% growth to 40,437 players. Capcom's secondary title gained +23% weekly. Sony drops 12% week-over-week, falling to 4,442 concurrent players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K reports on May 8; additional filings followed through June 12. Take-Two submitted a 10-K on May 22 and an 8-K on May 21. Electronic Arts filed a 10-K on May 11 and an 8-K on May 5. Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-Ks within the 14-day window.
