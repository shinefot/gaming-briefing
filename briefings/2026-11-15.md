# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-15

**Take-Two surges 76% on Steam as CD Projekt holds largest concurrent player base**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Ubisoft both posted 76% player growth over seven days, the sharpest gains tracked. CD Projekt commands the largest concurrent audience (109K players, +22%). Microsoft trades at $523.88; Take-Two at $227.82. Ubisoft stock lowest among major publishers at $0.94.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads Steam with ~109K concurrent players. Sega/Atlus (+47%), Paradox (+36%), and Capcom (+39%) all show double-digit growth. Electronic Arts trails at ~1,100 players (+63% weekly). All tracked publishers show positive weekly momentum.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K forms in the past 14 days (Oct 30–Nov 13); Microsoft submitted two 8-K filings and one 10-Q (Oct 28, Nov 4–5). Take-Two and Roblox each filed 8-K and 10-Q on their respective dates (Nov 9, Oct 29). No material disclosures evident from filing types alone.
