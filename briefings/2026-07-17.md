# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-17

**CD Projekt and Square Enix face sharp 8-day player declines on Steam.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads in concurrent players (~63k) but down 22% over 8 days. Square Enix dropped 32% (3.3k players); Sony fell 30% (3.4k). Take-Two held flat at ~42.8k. Stock moves modest: MSFT +1%, TTWO +1.7%, EA +0.8%; RBLX fell 7.2%, UBSFY down 0.8%.

## Community & Engagement
Broader decay across tracked titles. Capcom (-15%, 12.8k), Paradox (-11%, 15.5k), and Embracer (-8%, 32.2k) all down. Only Take-Two and Ubisoft held steady. CD Projekt's 22% slide is the steepest among large players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed 7 times in 14 days (six 6-K forms, one 20-F). Microsoft filed one 8-K (June 5). No other major publishers filed within the window.
