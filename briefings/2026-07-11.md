# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-11

**Square Enix and Capcom see steep Steam player declines; Take-Two alone posts gains.**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix down 24% over 8 days (lowest absolute decline tracked); Capcom fell 15%. Take-Two gained 1%, the only major publisher with positive momentum. Stock prices held flat or near-flat: Microsoft at $385.10, EA at $206.42, TTWO at $244.27. Ubisoft ticked up 3% on Steam but remains lowest-priced equity at $1.32.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt and Sega/Atlus each dropped 8% over the period. Paradox Interactive fell 11%. Ubisoft bucked the broader trend with +3% player growth. Take-Two's modest +1% gain stands alone among the larger publishers tracked.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports and one 20-F over the past 14 days, dominating disclosure activity. Microsoft, Roblox, and other majors each filed one 8-K or equivalent. No material details provided in filing summaries.
