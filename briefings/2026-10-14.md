# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-14

**Capcom surges 18% on Steam while Square Enix and EA plummet over 23%.**

## Market & Financials
Capcom stock holds steady at $14.13 despite Steam concurrent player gains of +18% over eight days—the strongest growth across tracked publishers. Take-Two (+8% players, $202.46 stock) and Embracer Group (+8% players, $8.77 stock) show aligned momentum. Square Enix drops -27% in player count and trades at $18.12; Electronic Arts falls -23% in players ($1.07 stock). Ubisoft trails at $1.08 stock with -9% player decline.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and Take-Two hold the largest Steam footprints (45k and 32k concurrent players respectively). CD Projekt declines -2% despite ranking third at 36k players. Sony (-14% players) and EA (-23%) show the steepest drops among top-10 tracked publishers. Paradox Interactive (-4%, 12k players) and Sega/Atlus (-1%, 24k players) remain relatively stable.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K forms over the past 14 days (Sept 3, 14, Oct 5, 14). Take-Two submitted two 8-K filings (Sept 22, 30). Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept 2). No material disclosures detailed in provided data.
