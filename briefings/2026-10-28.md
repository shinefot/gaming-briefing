# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-28

**CD Projekt surges 53% on Steam as Capcom tumbles 33%; Take-Two stock climbs despite player decline.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads Steam engagement with 141,003 concurrent players, up 53% over 7 days, while its stock holds at $17.11. Take-Two Interactive stock rose to $201.04 despite its 28,926 Steam players falling 15% weekly. Capcom's Steam presence crashed 33% to 5,229 players; stock dropped to $13.71. EA posted the only other weekly gain at +11% (1,069 players).

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and Sega/Atlus show modest declines (-20% and -4% respectively). The broader field—Paradox, Ubisoft, Sony, Square Enix—all declined 7–9% week-over-week. Only CD Projekt and EA bucked the trend.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-Ks (Oct. 5 and Oct. 14); Take-Two filed two 8-Ks (Sept. 30 and Sept. 22). No material details provided.
