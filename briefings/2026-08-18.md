# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-18

# **Embracer Surges 166% on Steam; EA Plummets 37% Over Eight Days**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group's Steam concurrent players jumped 166% to ~99k in eight days, commanding the largest player base among tracked publishers. EA dropped 37% to ~1.5k players, the steepest decline tracked. CD Projekt fell 25% to ~52k despite holding second place. Microsoft (MSFT) stock rose to $490.64; EA held flat at $209.70 despite player losses.

## Community & Engagement
Five of nine tracked publishers lost player share over eight days. Only Embracer and Ubisoft grew (+166% and +18% respectively). Capcom, Square Enix, and EA faced double-digit declines (−14%, −29%, −37%). Embracer and CD Projekt together account for ~153k concurrent players versus ~32k for the rest of the field.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the last 14 days (most recent 2026-08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, Electronic Arts, and Take-Two filed both 8-K/10-K or 10-Q documents. No notable filing themes evident from ticker data alone.
