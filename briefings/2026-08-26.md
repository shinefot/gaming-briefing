# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-26

# **Take-Two climbs +15% on Steam; EA surges +17% despite smallest player base.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Electronic Arts posted the strongest 7-day Steam gains at +15% and +17% respectively, though both rank mid-to-low in absolute concurrent players. Microsoft (MSFT) leads equity valuations at $494.94; Roblox (RBLX) trades at $39.64. CD Projekt stock dipped to $16.36 after a 7-day player decline of –2%.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the largest Steam footprint (~67k players) but faces a –6% weekly drop, the steepest decline tracked. Ubisoft trails at ~3k players, down –3% over seven days. Paradox Interactive remains flat at 12k+ players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony leads filing volume with five 6-K submissions over 14 days (most recent: 2026-08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, Take-Two, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and 10-Q/10-K documents; no material details provided in available data.
