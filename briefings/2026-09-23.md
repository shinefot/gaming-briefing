# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-23

**Square Enix surges 88% on Steam while legacy publishers face player declines**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix player count jumped 88% over seven days—the largest gain tracked. Ubisoft follows with +40%, while Embracer Group leads absolute concurrent players at 70,752 (+30%). Declines hit Take-Two (-9%), Sega/Atlus (-7%), and CD Projekt (-5%). Stock prices stable: MSFT $497–$499, EA $209.70, TTWO $205–$208.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and Paradox Interactive show sustained growth (+30%, +14%). Capcom and Sony hold flat (+2% each). Electronic Arts trails at 1,319 concurrent players (+8% over week). Ubisoft remains lowest in absolute count at 3,623 players despite strong percentage gain.
