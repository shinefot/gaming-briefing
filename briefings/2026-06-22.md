# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-22

**CD Projekt surges 78% in player count over 8 days; Ubisoft and Sony each slide 18–20%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt stock flat at $15.01; Microsoft leads at $379.44. Take-Two down slightly to $239.32 despite stable player base. Roblox tumbled from $51.55 to $46.57 despite no major player shifts. Ubisoft at $1.12 (lowest among tracked stocks).

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt commands two of the top three titles on Steam by concurrent players (70,786 and 48,283; +78% each). Embracer and Paradox hold steady with modest +1% to +23% gains. Ubisoft and Sony titles hemorrhaging players: −18% to −20% across four titles. Electronic Arts rebounded +64% in player count; Capcom flat.
