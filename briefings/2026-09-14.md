# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-14

**Take-Two and Square Enix see sharp player declines; Sony bucking trend with modest growth.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive leads Steam player declines at -19% over seven days, while Square Enix drops -20%. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $508.68; Take-Two (TTWO) at $221.24. Roblox (RBLX) shows the widest stock range in the data at $45.50–$50.89. Ubisoft (UBSFY) remains lowest-priced at $1.16.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the largest concurrent player base at 53,387 (-14% weekly). Sega/Atlus shows the smallest decline at -6%, while Sony is the only publisher with positive momentum at +2% over seven days. Paradox Interactive and Ubisoft players remain relatively stable (both -2% to -3%). Lower-engagement publishers (EA, Capcom, Square Enix, Ubisoft) all track below 10,000 concurrent players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports in the past 14 days (Sept 3 and Sept 14), plus three earlier filings. Take-Two filed both an 8-K and 10-Q on August 7. Microsoft's single 8-K is dated September 2.
