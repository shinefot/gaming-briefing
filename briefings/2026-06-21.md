# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-21

**CD Projekt and Capcom surge 160%+ on Steam; Sony filings spike to four in two weeks.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads player growth at +161% over seven days, followed by Capcom at +147% and Electronic Arts at +157%. Microsoft (MSFT) trades highest at $379.44; Ubisoft (UBSFY) lowest at $1.12. Take-Two Interactive (TTWO) at $239.32 holds second-largest valuation among tracked publishers.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt commands highest concurrent players (70,786), with Sega/Atlus (46,369) third. Take-Two (36,387) and Paradox (29,535) maintain mid-tier engagement. Ubisoft and Sony trail significantly (4,359 and 4,108 players respectively). All tracked companies except Sony show gains over seven days.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four documents in 14 days (6-K on 6/18, 6/12, 6/3; 20-F on 6/18)—heaviest filer. Take-Two (10-K on 5/22; 8-K on 5/21), Microsoft (8-K on 6/5, 5/14), Roblox (8-K on 5/29, 5/19), and Electronic Arts (10-K on 5/11) each filed once or twice. No filings from CD Projekt, Capcom, Embracer, Paradox, Square Enix, Sega/Atlus, or Ubisoft in this window.
