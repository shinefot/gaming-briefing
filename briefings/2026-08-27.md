# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-27

# **Capcom and EA hit 7-day lows; broad Steam player declines across all tracked publishers**

## Market & Financials
Capcom down 74% in concurrent players (9,758 to 2,475), the steepest drop among tracked titles. EA also sharp at -56% (1,459 to 723). Broader softness: all tracked publishers showing 7-day declines between -7% and -41%. Stock-wise, Microsoft MSFT leads at $505.06; Ubisoft UBSFY lowest at $1.25.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and CD Projekt hold largest Steam footprints (66,006 and 45,820 concurrent, respectively), but both shedding players at -30% and -35% over the week. Sony's titles show most resilience at -7% decline. Overall player concentration compressed: top two publishers represent ~36% of tracked concurrent volume.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K forms since mid-July, most recent on 2026-08-12. Microsoft submitted 10-K and 8-K on 2026-07-29. Take-Two, EA, and Roblox each filed 10-Q quarterly reports (late July to early August), with supporting 8-K forms.
