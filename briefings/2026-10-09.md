# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-09

**Steam concurrent players fall across all tracked publishers; Square Enix, EA, and Capcom post sharpest 6-day drops.**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft stock rose 2.5% to $534.84; Take-Two up 2.0% to $213.86. Roblox gained 5.8% to $48.51. Sega/Atlus declined 4.5% to $4.83; all others flat or minimal movement. Total concurrent Steam players down across the board, with Square Enix hardest hit at −45% over six days, followed by EA at −35% and Capcom at −32%. Sega/Atlus dropped −30%; Ubisoft −28%.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group leads concurrent count at ~41K players (−22%); CD Projekt and Take-Two follow with 39K and 32K respectively. Sony's portfolio shows the smallest decline at −9%. Paradox Interactive's 13K players fell −24%. No publisher recovered ground; all six-day trends are negative.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms (Sept 3 and Oct 5) and one Sept 14. Take-Two filed two 8-K forms (Sept 22 and Sept 30). Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept 2). No material event descriptions provided.
