# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-15

# **Take-Two slides 18% on Steam while Capcom gains 11%**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two's concurrent Steam players fell 18% over 7 days to ~34k, the steepest drop in the tracked set. Capcom bucked the trend with +11% growth to 6.4k players. Stock moves: Microsoft up to $508.68; Embracer Group climbed to $8.22 (up from $7.51); Take-Two at $221.24; Ubisoft at $1.18 (lowest among tracked equities).

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and CD Projekt each hold ~42k and 32k concurrent players respectively, both down 8% weekly. Paradox Interactive stable at –6%. Sony and Electronic Arts show modest gains (+7% and +26%) but from smaller bases. Square Enix down 14% to 2k players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms (Sept 3, Sept 14); Microsoft filed 8-K (Sept 2). Take-Two submitted 10-Q and 8-K on Aug 7. No material events disclosed in this summary.
