# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-16

**CD Projekt's Steam presence drops 21% in eight days as Take-Two gains 3%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt trails the field with the steepest decline among tracked publishers, down 21% over eight days to ~65k concurrent players. Take-Two holds the largest player base at ~42.6k, up 3%. Stock prices show modest movement; Microsoft ($397.64) and Take-Two ($242.81) lead by valuation, while Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.20–$1.25.

## Community & Engagement
Sony and Square Enix face sharper drops (–30% and –32% respectively) than most peers. Sega/Atlus and Embracer remain stable at ~34k and ~33k players with single-digit declines. Electronic Arts and Capcom show modest weakness (–13% and –4%). Paradox Interactive at 15.4k players is down 8%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed seven 6-K reports in the past two weeks (most recent 2026-07-14), plus one 20-F on 2026-06-18. Microsoft filed a single 8-K on 2026-06-05. No other tracked publishers filed in the period.
