# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-23

**CD Projekt and Square Enix post steepest Steam declines; Sony files heavily.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads player losses at -10% week-over-week (57,571 concurrent), while Square Enix trails at -18% (2,918). Take-Two and Sega/Atlus both posted +3% gains. Stock prices show modest movement: Microsoft up to $389.10, Roblox up to $50.53, but Ubisoft at $1.20 remains lowest-priced among tracked names.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt, Sony, and Electronic Arts all shed 10%+ Steam players over seven days. Take-Two's titles gained ground (+3%), as did Sega/Atlus. Ubisoft titles remain niche on Steam at 3,486 concurrent players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed nine 6-K forms and one 20-F within the past 14 days—substantially more activity than other tracked publishers. No material detail provided in filing list.
