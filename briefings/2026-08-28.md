# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-28

**Capcom and Ubisoft lead Steam declines; broad player pullback across major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Capcom's Steam concurrent players fell 72% over 7 days—steepest drop tracked. Ubisoft and Electronic Arts each dropped 48%; CD Projekt and Paradox fell 36% and 34% respectively. Only Sony held relatively steady at -16%. Stock prices mixed: Microsoft up to $513.53, Take-Two flat near $235, EA holding $209.70; Embracer, Sega, and Ubisoft at low valuations ($7.97, $4.71, $1.20).

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds largest concurrent player base (56,549), followed by CD Projekt (34,548) and Sega/Atlus (30,949). Take-Two (26,966) and Paradox (9,881) remain active. Capcom, Ubisoft, Sony, Square Enix, and EA all track below 3,000 concurrent players on Steam.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports within 14 days (last on 2026-08-12); Microsoft, Roblox, Take-Two, and EA each filed 8-K and quarterly updates (10-K or 10-Q) in late July through early August. No Material adverse disclosures flagged in brief filings summary.
