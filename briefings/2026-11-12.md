# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-12

# **Capcom leads 7-day player decline at –28%; Sony only gainer on Steam**

## Market & Financials
Capcom's Steam concurrent players fell 28% over seven days—steepest among tracked publishers. CD Projekt and Embracer each shed 20–21%; EA dropped 20%. Sony edged up 1%, alone in positive territory. Take-Two (–12%) and Ubisoft (–12%) fell moderately. Stock prices mostly flat: Microsoft $521.96, Take-Two $231.45, Roblox $53.57. Ubisoft at $0.98 remains lowest-priced.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt holds largest concurrent player base (~98k), followed by Embracer (~29k) and Take-Two (~25k). Sega/Atlus, Paradox, and Square Enix trail at sub-21k players. Ubisoft and Capcom trail sharply under 4k each. Across the portfolio, only Sony reversed a downward trend.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed nine 6-K reports over the past 14 days (last filed 2026-11-12). Microsoft submitted two 8-Ks, one 10-Q. Take-Two and Roblox each filed one 8-K and one 10-Q. No material details disclosed in this snapshot.
