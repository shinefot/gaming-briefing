# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-31

**Take-Two and Square Enix players down sharply; CD Projekt gains; Roblox stock volatile.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Paradox Interactive each shed 19% of Steam concurrent players over seven days, the steepest declines tracked. Square Enix dropped 34%. CD Projekt gained 3%, standing highest among tracked titles at ~61k concurrent players. Stock price movement: Roblox jumped to $48.20 (from $34.79), while Ubisoft fell to $1.29.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt holds the largest Steam concurrent player base. Electronic Arts posted the sole gain among major publishers (+2%), though at lowest absolute concurrent count (1.4k). Sony, Capcom, and Ubisoft all posted double-digit percentage drops over seven days.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent 31 July). Microsoft, Roblox, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and 10-Q/10-K filings on 29–30 July.
