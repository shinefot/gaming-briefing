# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-02

**Take-Two and Capcom lead Steam surge; broad player-count gains across portfolio.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and Capcom posted the strongest 7-day Steam growth at +89% and +103% respectively, though from smaller concurrent bases (42.5K and 4.85K players). Embracer Group holds the largest concurrent player pool at ~53.6K (+1%). Stock performance mixed: Microsoft at $500.36, Take-Two at $218.59, EA at $209.70. Ubisoft trails at $1.13; Embracer flat at $7.97.

## Community & Engagement
All tracked publishers show positive 7-day momentum. Sega/Atlus (+55%), CD Projekt (+26%), and Paradox Interactive (+31%) each gained meaningful concurrent players. EA (+39%) and Ubisoft (+54%) reversed slower engagement, though absolute concurrent counts remain low (941–3,049 players). Sony and Square Enix both at +20%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K forms (Aug 5, 11, 12, 31 July). Take-Two submitted 8-K and 10-Q on Aug 7. Microsoft filed 8-K and 10-K on July 29. Roblox posted 8-K and 10-Q on July 30. Microsoft, Roblox, and Take-Two disclosed quarterly results or material events in the past two weeks.
