# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-10

**Capcom and Square Enix surge on Steam while industry-wide player counts decline.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and CD Projekt stocks down 2.7% and 2.6% respectively over the tracking period. Capcom gained despite modest stock price movement. Roblox declined 3.7%, Embracer down 2.4%. Microsoft (MSFT) flat near $493.

## Community & Engagement
Capcom posted the only significant gain: **+20% concurrent players** over 8 days (6,514 peak). Square Enix also grew **+40%** (2,432 peak). Broad declines elsewhere: Embracer –15%, CD Projekt –13%, EA –17%. Take-Two and Sega/Atlus fell –9% and –11% respectively. Paradox Interactive's decline was modest at –5%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-Ks in the past 14 days (latest: Sept 3). Microsoft submitted two 8-Ks and one 10-K (July 29). Take-Two filed 8-K and 10-Q on Aug 7. Roblox filed 8-K and 10-Q on July 30.
