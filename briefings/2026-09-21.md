# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-21

**Square Enix and Ubisoft surge on Steam; Take-Two players drop 9% in a week.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive saw the steepest concurrent-player decline at –9% over seven days, while its stock held steady at $209.44. Square Enix and Ubisoft posted the strongest player growth: +66% and +40% respectively. Electronic Arts gained +19% concurrent players but trades at $209.70, level with Take-Two.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group leads concurrent players across tracked titles (53,421), followed by CD Projekt (42,745) and Sega/Atlus (42,653). All three saw modest declines (–2% to –4%). Paradox Interactive bucked the trend with +2% growth. Capcom and Sega/Atlus each dropped –4%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms in the past 14 days (Sept. 3 and Sept. 14), along with three earlier filings in August. Microsoft filed one 8-K on Sept. 2. No other major publishers disclosed recent SEC activity in this window.
