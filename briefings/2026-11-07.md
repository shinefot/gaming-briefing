# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-07

**CD Projekt sees steepest 7-day decline at -23%; Ubisoft, Capcom buck trend with player gains**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads downward pressure with concurrent players down 23% over seven days, while most publishers post single-digit losses. Ubisoft and Capcom each posted +10% and +6% gains respectively—the only gainers in the tracked set. Stock prices largely flat; Microsoft (MSFT) $533.27, Take-Two (TTWO) $216.55, and Roblox (RBLX) $49.30 show minimal movement.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt's two title positions (127.7K and 106.8K players) dominate raw concurrent volume but shed players faster than peers. Embracer Group, Take-Two, and Sega each hold two tracked titles. Sony's titles contracted 12% over the week and now sit among the lowest concurrent counts (2.7K–2.1K), alongside Electronic Arts (1.2K–946).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports between late October and early November. Microsoft and Roblox each filed quarterly 10-Q reports on October 29 and 28 respectively. Take-Two's most recent filing (8-K) dates to late September—no activity in the last 14 days.
