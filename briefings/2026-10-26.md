# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-26

**CD Projekt surges 227% on Steam as Capcom plummets 35%.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads Steam engagement with concurrent players up +227% over 7 days, commanding the highest player counts on the platform. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $531.97; Take-Two (TTWO) at $203.69. Capcom posts the steepest decline at -35% weekly, while Embracer Group and most major publishers show single-digit to mid-double-digit drops.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group concurrent players fell -24% week-over-week. Sega/Atlus held relatively flat (+1%), while Take-Two, Sony, Square Enix, and Electronic Arts each declined -10% to -11%. Paradox Interactive down -4%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Oct 5 and Oct 14) plus an earlier 6-K (Sept 14). Take-Two submitted two 8-K filings (Sept 30 and Sept 22). No material event details provided in filing summary.
