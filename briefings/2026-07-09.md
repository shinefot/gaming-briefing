# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-09

**Capcom and EA see steepest Steam declines; Ubisoft alone posts gains.**

## Market & Financials
Capcom and Electronic Arts both fell 29% and 24% over seven days on Steam, the largest drops in the tracked cohort. Ubisoft stood apart with a +6% gain. Stock prices showed modest moves: CD Projekt rose to $15.75, while Sega/Atlus dipped to $3.49. Microsoft (MSFT) traded near $383, Take-Two (TTWO) at $252.58.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt led concurrent players at ~84k, followed by Take-Two at ~43k. Sega/Atlus and Embracer Group each held ~35k. Paradox Interactive, Capcom, and Square Enix all saw double-digit percentage losses, ranging from 19% to 29%. Sony's Steam footprint remains minimal at under 5k players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed eight documents in the past 14 days, including a 20-F and multiple 6-Ks. Microsoft and Roblox each filed one 8-K. No material details disclosed in this snapshot.
