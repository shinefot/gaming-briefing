# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-14

**Square Enix surges 347% on Steam; Capcom posts 143% gain as console publishers drive week-over-week growth.**

## Market & Financials
All tracked publishers show positive 7-day player trends on Steam, ranging from +32% (Paradox Interactive) to +347% (Square Enix). Sega/Atlus leads in absolute concurrent players (49,484), followed by CD Projekt (42,665). Microsoft trades at $390.76; Take-Two at $211.77 and EA at $203.29 lead mid-tier valuations. Ubisoft at $1.07 remains lowest-priced among tracked equities.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix and Capcom show exceptional week-over-week momentum (+347% and +143%, respectively). Electronic Arts records the lowest concurrent player count (2,095 across tracked titles), while maintaining a +45% 7-day gain. All major publishers except Paradox Interactive (17,635 players) and Capcom (27,840) exceed 30,000 concurrent Steam players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony leads filing activity with six 6-K forms in the past 14 days. Take-Two filed both 8-K and 10-K documents (May 21–22). Microsoft and Roblox each submitted two 8-K filings. Electronic Arts and Microsoft filed 10-K and 8-K documents respectively in early May.
