# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-02

**Steam player counts down across the board, with Embracer and Square Enix hit hardest.**

## Market & Financials
All tracked publishers saw concurrent Steam players decline over the past week. Embracer Group and Square Enix posted the steepest drops at -34% and -33% respectively; CD Projekt's two tracked titles each fell -8%, the mildest decline. Microsoft (MSFT) trades at $516.53; Take-Two (TTWO) at $203.31. Sega/Atlus fell 2.9% to $5.18; Embracer held flat at $8.75.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group leads absolute concurrent players at 41,315, followed by CD Projekt at 35,477. Five publishers (Sony, Ubisoft, Embracer Group) report zero concurrent players on one tracked title each. Paradox Interactive, Capcom, and Ubisoft round out lower-engagement tiers at 13,703, 8,691, and 3,619 respectively.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Sept 3, Sept 14). Take-Two filed two 8-K reports (Sept 22, Sept 30). Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept 2). No specifics disclosed in this briefing.
