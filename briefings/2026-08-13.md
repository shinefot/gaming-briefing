# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-13

# **Embracer Group surges 176% in concurrent players; Sony and CD Projekt decline**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group's Steam titles lead with 146,751 and 91,833 concurrent players, both up 176% over 8 days. Stock flat at $7.20. Take-Two (244,341 total players, -7%) trades at $244.02; Electronic Arts (4,443 players, +13%) at $209.70. Ubisoft holds lowest player count (6,356 total, +3%) at $1.18 stock price.

## Community & Engagement
Sony's titles drop 14% to 4,316 players; CD Projekt falls 10% to 121,192 players. Sega/Atlus, Paradox, and Capcom each decline 4–6%. Square Enix (6,179 players, +10%) and Electronic Arts (+13%) are lone gainers outside Embracer.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed seven 6-K reports in the last 14 days (most recent 2026-08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, Electronic Arts, and Take-Two each filed 8-K and 10-Q filings. No material detail provided in filing types alone.
