# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-01

**EA surges 45% on Steam concurrent players; Embracer down 19%**

## Market & Financials
Electronic Arts leads 7-day player growth at +45%, though absolute concurrent count remains lowest among tracked publishers at 1,406. CD Projekt holds largest player base at 165,502 (+13%). Stock prices stable across major holdings; Microsoft leads at $521.91, Ubisoft lowest at $0.87.

## Community & Engagement
Smaller publishers driving engagement gains: Ubisoft (+35%), Sony (+36%), and Square Enix (+28%) all outpacing CD Projekt's growth rate. Embracer Group sole decliner at -19% concurrent players. Take-Two (+19%) and Capcom (+29%) post mid-range growth.

## Regulatory Filings
Microsoft filed 10-Q and dual 8-K notices (10/28); Sony submitted five 6-K filings over past two weeks; Roblox filed 10-Q and 8-K (10/29); Take-Two's most recent filings dated late September.
