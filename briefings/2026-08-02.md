# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-02

**CD Projekt surges +17% on Steam as Take-Two and Square Enix slip.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads 8-day player growth at +17%, reaching 76,147 concurrent players. Electronic Arts posts the second-strongest gain (+20%) from a smaller base of 2,231 players. Take-Two and Square Enix both decline sharply: -13% and -16% respectively. Microsoft trades at $464.72; Take-Two at $242.92; EA at $209.86.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt and Embracer Group are the only top-tier publishers with positive momentum. Paradox Interactive, Sega/Atlus, and Sony all show 4–9% declines over eight days. Ubisoft remains lowest among tracked publishers with 4,289 concurrent players (-6%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K forms between late June and July 31; Microsoft and Roblox each filed 8-K and 10-Q/10-K pairs on July 29–30. Electronic Arts filed an 8-K on July 30. No notable disclosure patterns emerge from filing types alone.
