# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-25

**CD Projekt surges 306% in weekly Steam concurrent players, driving broad engagement gains across tracked publishers.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads with 177,502 concurrent players (+306% weekly). All tracked publishers show gains: Capcom posts the second-strongest growth at +64%, while Ubisoft and Electronic Arts lag at +27% and +29% respectively. Stock prices stable across the board; Microsoft at $524.51, Take-Two at $203.69, Ubisoft at $0.93.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds second-place concurrent player count at 62,762 (+38%). Take-Two, Sega/Atlus, and Paradox Interactive cluster in the 30–46k range with +30% to +36% weekly growth. Lowest engagement: Electronic Arts (1,720 players) and Sony (3,496 players), both still positive week-over-week.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed three 6-K reports (Oct 14, Oct 5, Sept 14). Take-Two issued two 8-K filings (Sept 30, Sept 22). No material disclosures noted in filing types provided.
