# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-01

**Sega/Atlus dominates Steam concurrency while Ubisoft stock trades at lowest valuation among tracked peers.**

## Market & Financials
Sega/Atlus leads Steam concurrent players at 26,769, followed by Embracer Group (20,984) and Take-Two (19,414). Stock valuations: MSFT $460.52 trades highest; Ubisoft (UBSFY) at $1.27 is lowest among tracked gaming companies. Take-Two stock ($226.98) outpaces EA ($202.00) despite trailing on Steam concurrency.

## Community & Engagement
Sega/Atlus, Embracer, and Take-Two command ~66K combined concurrent Steam players—two-thirds of the tracked total. Bottom tier (Capcom, Ubisoft, Sony, Square Enix, EA) collectively holds under 7K players. Mid-tier Paradox Interactive (10,598) stands notably ahead of larger publishers by this metric.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports in the past 14 days (May 8–19), the highest volume among tracked filers. Take-Two and Roblox each filed two 8-Ks; Take-Two also filed a 10-K on May 22. EA filed 8-K and 10-K; Microsoft and Roblox each filed single 8-Ks. No material disclosures noted in filing types alone.
