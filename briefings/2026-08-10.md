# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-10

# **Square Enix surges 55% in Steam players; Take-Two and Sony decline**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads at $508.53; Take-Two at $252.48 and EA at $209.70 hold mid-range positions. Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.19. Square Enix and Sony show stock price stability despite player count volatility.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt commands the largest concurrent player base (80,180), up 10% over 8 days. Square Enix posts the sharpest gain: +55% across tracked titles. Conversely, Sony and Ubisoft shed players: −16% and −11% respectively. Sega/Atlus and Paradox Interactive flat or declining.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent 2026-08-05). Microsoft, Roblox, Electronic Arts, and Take-Two each submitted 8-K and quarterly filings. No 10-K filings beyond Microsoft's (2026-07-29).
