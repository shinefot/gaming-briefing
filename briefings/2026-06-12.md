# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-12

**Square Enix surges 86% on Steam as industry-wide player declines persist.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two (TTWO: $212.03) and EA (EA: $203.08) lead by stock price, but both saw Steam concurrent players drop 22–33% over seven days. Microsoft (MSFT: $387.78) remains highest-priced. Sega/Atlus, CD Projekt, and Embracer all shed 10–24% of players week-over-week.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix's twin titles posted +86% Steam growth—the only major outlier in a sector-wide contraction. Sega/Atlus titles held the highest concurrent counts (35k–39k), while EA's offerings fell hardest (−33%). Paradox Interactive dropped 24%, Embracer 21%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K forms on 2026-05-08 alone, plus three additional filings through mid-June. Take-Two released a 10-K (2026-05-22) alongside an 8-K (2026-05-21). Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-Ks; EA submitted a 10-K and one 8-K.
