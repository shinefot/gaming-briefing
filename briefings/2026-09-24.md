# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-24

# **Square Enix surges 45% on Steam; Capcom plummets 30% in weekly player drop**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads valuations at $499.34; Take-Two at $208.74. Stock prices show modest movement, with Sega/Atlus down to $4.97 and Ubisoft at $1.19.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group dominates concurrent players (~54K–53K), up 15% weekly. Square Enix posts the week's strongest growth at +45%, though from a smaller base (3.3K–3.2K players). Capcom faces the steepest decline at -30% (6.9K–6.2K). Sega/Atlus, Take-Two, and CD Projekt all down 10–19% week-over-week.

Sony filed two 6-K reports (Sept 3, Sept 14); Microsoft posted 8-K (Sept 2); Take-Two filed 8-K (Sept 22). Sony also has an earlier 6-K from Aug 12.
