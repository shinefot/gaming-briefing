# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-30

**Capcom and Square Enix post steepest Steam declines; broad player pullback across major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Capcom and Square Enix led declines with –59% and –52% 7-day drops in concurrent Steam players respectively. Embracer Group (–39%), Take-Two (–37%), and Electronic Arts (–38%) also saw significant outflows. Roblox stock fell 19% to $41.82; Microsoft held near $520; Ubisoft trading at $0.91.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt retained the highest concurrent player count at 136k, though down 14% week-over-week. Embracer's portfolio trails at 38k players (–39%). All tracked publishers show negative 7-day trends; smallest decline is Ubisoft at –24%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (Oct 5–30). Microsoft and Roblox each filed quarterly updates and current events reports (Oct 28–29). Take-Two submitted two 8-K filings in September. No material disclosures summarized in filing headers.
