# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-29

**Embracer Group's Steam portfolio bleeding players at twice the rate of industry peers.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group leads in concurrent players (46K–41K) but faces steepest declines: –24% over 7 days, double the average weakness. Most other tracked publishers are flat to slightly down (–0% to –7%). Paradox Interactive is the outlier: +12% gain. Stock prices show mixed movement; Roblox dropped to $41.40, while Microsoft held above $507.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and Capcom (–19%) are shedding players fastest. CD Projekt, Sega/Atlus, and Take-Two show moderate single-digit declines (–4% to –7%). Ubisoft and Electronic Arts post small gains (+2%). Paradox's +12% growth is the only material positive trend in the tracked set.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-Ks (Sept. 3 & 14); Microsoft and Take-Two each filed 8-Ks (Sept. 2 & 22). No material disclosures flagged in summaries.
