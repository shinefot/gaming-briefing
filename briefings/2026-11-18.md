# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-18

# **Take-Two gains Steam share as most publishers slide week-over-week**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two's Steam concurrent players rose 10% over seven days to ~24K, the strongest trend among tracked publishers. CD Projekt and Embracer faced the steepest declines (-15% and -17% respectively). Stock performance mixed: Take-Two +6.4% (TTWO $203.93→$216.98), Roblox +7.0% (RBLX $44.00→$47.10), Ubisoft -3.2% (UBSFY $0.90→$0.93). Microsoft and Sony stocks flat to up slightly.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads concurrent players (~74K), but Square Enix posted the second-strongest 7-day trend at +18% despite lower absolute numbers (~1.4K). Sega/Atlus (-3%) and Electronic Arts (+5%) showed minimal movement. Sony's engagement flat-lined at ~2K concurrent players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K forms between late October and mid-November; Microsoft issued two 8-Ks and a 10-Q (Oct 28); Take-Two and Roblox both filed 10-Qs on Nov 9 and Oct 29 respectively. No material disclosures flagged in the filing types noted.
