# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-31

**Steam engagement drops across all major publishers; EA alone posts weekly gains.**

## Market & Financials
Broad player-count decline dominates the week: Embracer Group and Sony down 20–21%, Square Enix down 22%, while Take-Two and Ubisoft fall 14–16%. EA is the sole outlier, up 6% over seven days. Stock prices show mixed movement; MSFT and TTWO stable, but SQNXF, CCOEY, and SGAMY each down 2–5% intraday.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads concurrent players at 159,656 (−10%), followed by Embracer at 44,478 (−20%). Paradox, Capcom, and Sega/Atlus remain well below 30K concurrent. Ubisoft and Sony titles cluster at the lowest end (under 3.5K concurrent).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K forms in October. Microsoft and Roblox each submitted 10-Q quarterly reports on 28–29 October, alongside 8-K notifications. Take-Two filed two 8-Ks in late September.
