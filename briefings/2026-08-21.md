# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-21

**Embracer Group down 35% in concurrent players over 8 days; broad Steam engagement decline across major publishers.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive stock fell 3.6% to $243.22; Electronic Arts flat at $209.70. Microsoft (MSFT) edged down to $482.15. Roblox (RBLX) declined 2.4% to $38.74. Ubisoft remains lowest-priced at $1.24, down 2.4%.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group faces steepest player drop at -35% over 8 days (72,730 current). CD Projekt down 17% (48,061 players). Square Enix (-26%), Electronic Arts (-23%), and Capcom (-14%) also show double-digit declines. Paradox Interactive and Sega/Atlus hold steady near flat. Embracer and CD Projekt command largest concurrent audiences among tracked companies.
