# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-25

**Electronic Arts surges 26% in concurrent players; Embracer and Square Enix both fall 9%.**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive stock rose to $240.51, outpacing peers. Electronic Arts gained 26% in Steam concurrent players over 7 days—the largest week-over-week move in the tracked set. Conversely, Embracer Group and Square Enix each dropped 9%, the steepest declines tracked.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the largest Steam player base at 67,394 concurrent. CD Projekt trails at ~46,700, while Sega/Atlus, Take-Two, and Paradox each hold 12,000–33,000. Ubisoft (3,254) and Square Enix (2,230) show the smallest concurrent populations in this group.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports over 14 days (dates: 7/14, 7/17, 7/31, 8/5, 8/11–12). Microsoft, Roblox, Take-Two, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and 10-Q/10-K pairs. No material details provided in today's snapshot.
