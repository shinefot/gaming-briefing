# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-29

**CD Projekt and Take-Two surge on Steam; Sony files heavily.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt's concurrent players jumped 42% over 8 days to lead the tracker at 87,289. Take-Two Interactive also climbed 46% to 45,957 players. TTWO stock stands at $244.76; MSFT at $372.97. Roblox (RBLX) stock dipped to $54.25 from $47.56 earlier in the period.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and Paradox Interactive both declined: Embracer -7%, Paradox -11%. Sony's titles posted the steepest growth at +71%, though from a smaller base (6,321 players). Ubisoft and EA remain lowest on the concurrent player list at 4,257 and 2,668 respectively.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five times in the past 14 days (four 6-K forms, one 20-F annual). Take-Two submitted a 10-K and 8-K in May. Microsoft and Roblox each filed one 8-K.
