# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-23

# **Paradox Interactive posts strongest 7-day Steam surge at +25%; Capcom alone shows decline.**

## Market & Financials
Paradox Interactive leads Steam growth at +25% over 7 days (15,277 concurrent players). Sony and Ubisoft follow at +32% and +23% respectively, though from smaller player bases. Capcom is the outlier, down 4% to ~10,050 players. Stock valuations range widely: Microsoft at $483.24 vs. Ubisoft at $1.19.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the largest concurrent player base (82,910), followed by CD Projekt (57,905). Take-Two, Sega/Atlus, and Paradox all show double-digit gains. Capcom's flat-to-negative performance stands apart among tracked publishers.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (07-14 through 08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, Take-Two, and Electronic Arts all submitted 8-K and 10-Q filings mid-to-late July/early August. Take-Two's most recent 10-Q is from 08-07.
