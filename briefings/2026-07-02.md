# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-02

# **Take-Two surges 43% in Steam players; Sony files six reports in eight days**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two Interactive leads Steam growth at +43% over eight days (41,005 concurrent players), while Sony stock rose to $20.73. Microsoft holds the highest share price at $389.30. Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.19, with Electronic Arts at $205.79 and Roblox at $58.11.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt tops concurrent players (79,816; +18%), followed by Take-Two. Sony shows the sharpest gains at +82% despite lower absolute numbers (5,065 players). Paradox Interactive and Square Enix declined 9% and 15% respectively. Sega/Atlus held flat at 38,207 players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed seven reports in 14 days—six 6-K forms and one 20-F annual report. Microsoft, Take-Two, and Roblox each filed 8-K or annual disclosures. Take-Two's 10-K was filed May 22.
