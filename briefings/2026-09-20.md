# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-20

**Electronic Arts surges 63% on Steam in 8 days; Take-Two slides 14% as Embracer holds steady.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group dominates Steam concurrent players (~54K), flat over eight days. Take-Two Interactive trails at ~42K but has shed 14% of its player base; CD Projekt and Sega/Atlus both down 6–7%. Stock prices remain steady: Microsoft leads at $493.78; EA at $209.70; Take-Two at $205.45. Ubisoft trades lowest among listed publishers at $1.17.

## Community & Engagement
Capcom and Electronic Arts post the sharpest gains: +34% and +63% respectively over eight days. Square Enix climbs 50% to 4,900 concurrent players. Paradox Interactive, Sony, and CD Projekt all decline modestly (–4% to –6%). Embracer's titles show no net movement.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K forms within three days (Sept. 11–14), plus two earlier filings (Aug. 11–12). Microsoft filed a single 8-K on Sept. 2. No other tracked publishers have SEC filings in the past 14 days.
