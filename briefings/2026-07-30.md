# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-30

**CD Projekt surges 9% on Steam while Square Enix drops 17% in week-over-week concurrent players.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt stock rose to $16.33, while broader market weakness hit Take-Two (TTWO $249.05), EA ($209.10), and Ubisoft ($1.23). Microsoft remains highest-priced at $447.23.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt leads concurrent players at ~59,900 (+9% weekly), followed by Take-Two at ~37,900 (-13%). Square Enix fell sharpest at -17% weekly. Ubisoft and EA hold lowest player counts among tracked publishers.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six documents over 14 days, including a 20-F and multiple 6-Ks. Microsoft filed 8-K and 10-K on July 29. No other major publishers disclosed recent filings in this window.
