# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-05

**Capcom faces steepest Steam decline at -49% week-over-week; Ubisoft sole gainer at +1%.**

## Market & Financials
Broad Steam player declines across most publishers. Capcom's -49% drop is the largest; CD Projekt, Electronic Arts, and Take-Two all down 22–24%. Ubisoft stands alone with +1% growth. Stock prices show mixed movement: Microsoft up to $558.04, Take-Two at $212.18, but Ubisoft weak at $0.93 (up from $0.87).

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt holds the highest concurrent player count at 113,734 but reflects the -24% weekly trend. Embracer Group second at 33,741 players. All tracked publishers except Ubisoft show negative week-over-week engagement. Capcom and Paradox Interactive the only single-digit player bases among those listed.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports over the past 14 days (Oct 5–Nov 5). Microsoft submitted two 8-Ks and one 10-Q (Oct 28–Nov 5). Roblox filed an 8-K and 10-Q on Oct 29. Take-Two submitted one 8-K on Sept 30. No material details disclosed in this snapshot.
