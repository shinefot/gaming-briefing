# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-20

# **Square Enix surges 67% on Steam while most publishers decline; Take-Two outpaces peers with +16% player growth**

## Market & Financials
Square Enix stock rose to $23.40, tracking a 67% player uptick on Steam over seven days. Take-Two Interactive gained 16% in concurrent players and holds $210.04 stock price. Microsoft trades at $498.30; Ubisoft remains lowest among tracked stocks at $0.91.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt, Embracer, Sega/Atlus, and Paradox Interactive all posted 22–25% player declines over the week. Capcom fell 41% (steepest drop), while Sony and Electronic Arts bucked the trend with +34% and +19% gains respectively. CD Projekt holds the largest absolute player base at 79,120 concurrent users.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K reports across four dates (Oct 30–Nov 13); Microsoft submitted 10-Q and two 8-K filings (Oct 28–Nov 5). Take-Two filed 10-Q and 8-K on Nov 9, with an additional 8-K on Nov 20. Roblox and Microsoft also submitted quarterly reports (10-Q) in late October.
