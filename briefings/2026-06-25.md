# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-25

# **CD Projekt surges 119% on Steam; Embracer and Capcom decline sharply**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads Steam with 67,857 concurrent players, up 119% over 8 days. Microsoft stock rose to $374.80; Take-Two and EA stocks also gained. Roblox recovered to $49.12 after earlier weakness. Ubisoft remains lowest-priced at $1.14, down from $1.07 earlier.

## Community & Engagement
EA shows strong momentum: +57% player growth over 8 days despite low absolute numbers (2,317 players). Sega/Atlus flat at +6%. Embracer Group and Capcom both declined steeply (−17% and −15% respectively). Sony, Ubisoft, and Paradox each lost 10–14% player share.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed most heavily: six documents in 14 days (four 6-Ks, one 20-F, one 6-K). Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-Ks. Take-Two filed a 10-K (annual report) and an 8-K. No notable public guidance changes evident from filing types alone.
