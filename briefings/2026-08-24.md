# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-24

**Embracer Group hemorrhages 36% of Steam players in one week; EA and Paradox buck downtrend.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group tops concurrent player counts but faces steepest decline at –36% over seven days. Microsoft (MSFT) leads stock valuations at $486.56; Take-Two (TTWO) trades at $240.51. EA and Paradox Interactive are lone gainers, each +1% to +4% weekly player growth; all other tracked publishers negative.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer's two tracked titles hold largest absolute audiences (82,910 and 63,296 players) yet show sharpest engagement drop. CD Projekt and Capcom both down –8%; Square Enix steeper at –10%. Ubisoft smallest player base (3,254–4,382) and declining –7%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports within the past 14 days (most recent 2026-08-12). Microsoft, Roblox, Take-Two, and Electronic Arts each submitted 8-K and 10-K/10-Q pairs; EA added a second 8-K on 2026-08-04. No material details disclosed in this briefing.
