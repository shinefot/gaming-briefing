# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-17

# **Embracer Group surges +122% on Steam in 8 days; CD Projekt and EA both down sharply.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group commands the largest Steam concurrent player base (93,339), up 122% over 8 days. CD Projekt dropped 25% to 52,842 players; Electronic Arts fell 30% to 1,515 players. Stock prices are largely flat: Microsoft at $495.40, Take-Two at $246.95, EA at $209.70. Ubisoft shows the only engagement bright spot besides Embracer, gaining 17% to 4,670 players, though its stock remains the lowest at $1.22.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer and Ubisoft are the only tracked publishers with player growth over 8 days. Seven of nine publishers show declining concurrent players. CD Projekt, Square Enix (−25%), EA (−30%), and Capcom (−12%) face the steepest drops. Sega/Atlus (40,208 players) remains second-largest by count despite a −5% decline.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K forms across July–August; Microsoft, Roblox, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and 10-Q documents in late July/early August. Take-Two submitted 8-K and 10-Q filings on August 7th. No material transaction disclosures noted in available summaries.
