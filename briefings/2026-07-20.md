# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-07-20

**CD Projekt and Square Enix player counts down sharply; Sony filing activity elevated.**

CD Projekt (-16% over 8 days) and Square Enix (-20%) show the steepest player declines among tracked publishers. Take-Two Interactive gains 3% concurrent players, highest positive momentum. Stock prices mostly flat; Microsoft at $395.89, Take-Two at $241.02, EA at $209.08.

## Regulatory Filings
