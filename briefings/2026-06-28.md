# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-28

# **CD Projekt and Take-Two surge on Steam; Sony files burst of regulatory reports**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads concurrent-player growth at +23% over 8 days (87K players), followed by Take-Two at +26% (46K players). Stock prices stable: MSFT $373.01, TTWO $238.57, EA $205.29. Embracer (-11%), Paradox (-16%), and Square Enix (-7%) post declines. Sony's player count surged +54% (6.3K players) despite modest stock price ($19.72).

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt and Take-Two hold the largest concurrent-player bases among tracked publishers. Sega/Atlus steady at 47K (+3%). Ubisoft and EA track at the low end of engagement (4.3K and 3K players respectively), both flat to slightly positive over 8 days.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports and one 20-F (annual) in the past 14 days—highest activity among tracked companies. Take-Two submitted a 10-K and 8-K (May 22 and May 21). Roblox and Microsoft each filed one 8-K. No other publishers filed in this window.
