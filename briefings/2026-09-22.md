# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-22

# **Embracer and Square Enix surge on Steam; Take-Two and CD Projekt slip**

## Market & Financials
Embracer Group leads Steam engagement with two titles hitting 70K+ concurrent players (+79% weekly), while Square Enix posts the second-strongest weekly gain at +83% despite lower absolute player counts (3.3–3.4K). Take-Two and CD Projekt both decline slightly week-over-week (−1% to −6%), though both maintain 30K+ player bases. Stock-wise: TTWO gained to $209.44 (from $205.61); EA and MSFT hold steady; RBLX dipped to $49.71 (from $51.00); Ubisoft flat at $1.17.

## Community & Engagement
Ubisoft and Capcom show resilience with +46% and +14% weekly climbs, though absolute counts remain low (3.7K and 7.4K respectively). Paradox Interactive holds steady at +3%. Sega/Atlus, despite flat-to-negative trends (−5%), retains the third-largest player base at 30K+. Sony's PC presence minimal (2.3K players, +6%).
