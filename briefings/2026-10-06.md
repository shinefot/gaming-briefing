# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-06

**Capcom surges 81% in concurrent players; CD Projekt and Sony also post double-digit gains over six days.**

## Market & Financials
Microsoft leads by valuation at $530.54; Take-Two Interactive trades at $202.64 with 32,368 concurrent Steam players (+17%). Ubisoft trails at $1.09 stock price and 3,509 players (-5% over six days). Embracer Group holds the largest player base (41,444) but shows minimal growth (+3%).

## Community & Engagement
Capcom's 81% player surge stands out, reaching 9,504 concurrent users. CD Projekt (+42%, 39,625 players) and Sony (+54%, 3,250 players) also post strong gains. Conversely, Sega/Atlus (-6%) and Square Enix (-8%) declined over the period.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed three 6-K forms (Sept 3, Sept 14, Oct 5); Take-Two filed two 8-Ks (Sept 22, Sept 30); Microsoft filed one 8-K (Sept 2). No material details disclosed in this snapshot.
