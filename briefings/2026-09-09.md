# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-09

**Square Enix jumps 35% in Steam concurrent players; EA drops 19%**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two leads concurrent players at 43K–41K, with Microsoft (MSFT) at $493 and Take-Two (TTWO) at $215 among tracked stocks. Roblox (RBLX) moved to $45.55 from $43.93; Ubisoft (UBSFY) remains at $1.08, lowest in the group.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix posts the week's strongest gain: +35% to 2.5K players. Capcom also grew +7% to 5.8K. Across the field, declines dominate: Embracer, Take-Two, CD Projekt, Sega/Atlus, and Paradox all lost 5–11%. Electronic Arts fell hardest at −19% to 885 players.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports over 14 days (most recent 2026-09-03). Microsoft and Roblox each submitted 8-K and quarterly filings. Take-Two filed 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-08-07. No material details disclosed in provided data.
