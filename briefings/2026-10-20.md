# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-20

**Embracer Group surges 52% on Steam; Square Enix down 22% in sharp eight-day divergence.**

## Market & Financials
Embracer leads with 69,026 concurrent players (+52% over 8 days), followed by CD Projekt at 44,749 (+27%). Take-Two holds steady at ~32,880 (+7%). Stock moves are modest: Microsoft at $534.21, Take-Two at $205.79, Roblox at $52.34. Sega/Atlus stock fell to $4.71 (−$0.30); Square Enix flat at $18.12.

## Community & Engagement
Square Enix player count collapsed to 1,937 (−22% over 8 days), the steepest decline tracked. Sony also down 14% to 2,676 players. Ubisoft remains the smallest tracked title at 2,942 players (−4%). Capcom shows solid traction: 6,777 concurrent players (+13%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed two 6-K reports (Oct 5 and 14) and one earlier (Sept 14). Take-Two filed two 8-K notifications (Sept 22 and 30). No material disclosures detailed in submissions provided.
