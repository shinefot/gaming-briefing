# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-08-01

**CD Projekt surges +16% on Steam while Take-Two and Square Enix slide.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads concurrent player growth at +16% over 8 days (69,704 peak), while Electronic Arts shows the second-strongest momentum at +22% (2,231 players). Take-Two and Square Enix face headwinds: Take-Two down 12% (40,803 players), Square Enix down 19% (2,810 players). Stock moves are muted: Microsoft up 2.5%, Sega up 6.2%, but Ubisoft flat and CD Projekt up 1.3%.

## Community & Engagement
CD Projekt and Embracer maintain the largest player bases on Steam among tracked companies. Paradox Interactive, Capcom, and Ubisoft show modest engagement declines (−2% to −7%). Sony's titles trail significantly (2,684–3,143 concurrent players, down 11%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed five 6-K reports in the past 14 days (most recent 7/31). Microsoft, Roblox, and Electronic Arts each filed 8-K and quarterly/annual updates (7/29–7/30). No material disclosures flagged in filing types.
