# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-11-06

**Steam engagement collapses across the board; Capcom down 59% in one week.**

## Market & Financials
Broad player-count decline: all tracked publishers lost 25–59% of concurrent Steam players over seven days, with Capcom hardest hit at -59%. CD Projekt holds the largest player base (106k), followed by Embracer (32k). Stock moves are mixed: Take-Two +2%, Microsoft +0.4%, Ubisoft -3.2%, Capcom -4% (on the week).

## Community & Engagement
No title is holding momentum. Ubisoft's decline is lowest at -1%, but baseline players remain minimal (3.2k). Paradox, Sega/Atlus, and Sony each shed 25–29%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed six 6-K forms in the past 14 days (latest Nov 5). Microsoft submitted 10-Q and three 8-K filings; Roblox filed 10-Q and 8-K on Oct 29. Take-Two's most recent 8-K is dated Sept 30 (outside the two-week window).
