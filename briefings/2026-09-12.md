# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-12

# **Capcom surges 51% on Steam while Take-Two and CD Projekt face double-digit player declines**

## Market & Financials
Take-Two and CD Projekt show the steepest 7-day player drops at -12% and -11% respectively, with concurrent players in the 36–51k range. Capcom posts the only major engagement gain at +51%, though from a smaller base (8–12k players). Stock prices remain stable across tracked publishers; Microsoft leads at $495.69, while Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.08–$1.16.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group and Sega/Atlus hold the largest Steam audiences (38–61k concurrent), but both face modest headwinds (-9% and -2%). Paradox Interactive and Square Enix show flat to minimal movement. Electronic Arts +16% gain signals modest growth from a small player base (1–1.5k).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony has filed six 6-K reports in 14 days (most recent Sept. 3). Take-Two issued both 8-K and 10-Q on Aug. 7. Roblox filed 8-K and 10-Q on July 30. Microsoft's Aug. 2 8-K is the sole recent filing among major publishers outside Sony's recent activity.
