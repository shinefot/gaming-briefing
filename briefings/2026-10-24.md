# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-10-24

# **CD Projekt surges 163% on Steam; Sony and Square Enix decline sharply**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads engagement with 158,342 concurrent players (+163% over 7 days), while Take-Two holds the second-largest base at 46,231 players but declined 3%. Sony and Square Enix face the steepest losses: Sony down 20%, Square Enix down 16%. Stock prices show modest moves; CD Projekt traded $17.13–$17.42, Take-Two $203.69–$206.32. Ubisoft trades lowest at $0.93–$0.99.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group's titles remain stable at 62,762 players (+28%). Capcom (14,695), Paradox Interactive (16,614), and Sega/Atlus (34,381) show flat to negligible weekly changes. Electronic Arts posts modest growth at 1,720 players (+10%), while Ubisoft trails at 3,548 players (−13%).

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed three 6-K disclosures since early September; Take-Two filed two 8-K forms (September 22 and 30). No material details provided in available summaries.
