# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-09-11

# **Steam engagement drops across the board; Square Enix down 37% week-over-week**

## Market & Financials
All tracked publishers show concurrent player declines over seven days, with Square Enix hardest hit at -37%. Embracer Group and Take-Two follow at -32% and -29% respectively. Stock prices hold relatively steady: Microsoft leads at $495.69; Take-Two at $219.29; EA at $209.70. Ubisoft trades lowest at $1.10.

## Community & Engagement
Embracer Group holds the largest concurrent player base (47,709), followed by Take-Two (40,902) and Sega/Atlus (38,252). Capcom shows the smallest decline at -16%, while Paradox Interactive and Ubisoft hold mid-range drops (-23% and -24%). Electronic Arts and Square Enix represent the lowest absolute player counts among major publishers.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-Ks in the past 14 days (most recent 2026-09-03). Take-Two submitted both 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-08-07. Microsoft filed two 8-Ks and one 10-K; Roblox filed 8-K and 10-Q on 2026-07-30. No material event summaries provided.
