# Gaming Industry Briefing — 2026-06-20

**CD Projekt and Paradox surge on Steam; EA rebounds 76% while Take-Two and Sony decline.**

## Market & Financials
CD Projekt leads concurrent players at 63,988 (+50% over 7 days); Paradox Interactive posts the sharpest gain at +66%. Take-Two and Sony both show declines: Take-Two down 11%, Sony down 27% week-over-week. EA rebounds sharply at +76% despite modest absolute player count (3,079). Stock prices flat today across all tracked companies.

## Community & Engagement
Ubisoft (4,001 players, -19%) and Sony (3,718 players, -27%) face the steepest player losses among tracked titles. Embracer Group holds steady at +21% growth. Square Enix and Capcom each posted minor 3% declines. Paradox and EA show the only double-digit gains above 50%.

## Regulatory Filings
Sony filed four 6-K reports and one 20-F (annual) within the past 14 days—highest frequency among filers. Microsoft and Roblox each filed two 8-K notices. Take-Two filed 10-K (annual) on May 22 and 8-K on May 21. EA published 10-K on May 11. No other companies filed in the window.
